# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Local and regional business owners — shops, guesthouses, restaurants, and small service companies — often non-technical, busy, and deciding on a phone or laptop between other work. They need a studio that feels trustworthy enough to hand over their online presence.

Primary job: understand what IJ Technology builds, see proof it works for businesses like theirs, pick a clear package, and send a contact message (or call/email) without friction.

## Product Purpose

IJ Technology is a bilingual (English / Albanian) marketing landing page for a web studio that designs and builds websites, online shops, and custom digital products. Success is a confident visitor who trusts the studio and converts via the contact form or direct contact channels.

## Brand Personality

Clear · capable · approachable, with Serious · precise · premium restraint. Confidence first — not hype, not cartoon energy as the lead signal. Voice should feel like a skilled local partner who ships carefully, explains plainly, and never talks down to the client.

Emotional goal: “These people can deliver something solid and tailored for my business.”

## Anti-references

- Quick AI-generated / sloppy agency landing pages (generic dark neon, purple-glow SaaS, identical icon-card grids, gradient text everywhere, empty “cinematic” chrome without substance)
- Cheap freelancing-marketplace vibes
- Cold corporate enterprise brochures that feel distant from local SMEs

## Design Principles

1. **Confidence over spectacle** — Motion and craft should prove capability; they must never outrank clarity, pricing honesty, or the path to contact.
2. **Show the work, then the offer** — Real projects and outcomes before package choice; proof earns the ask.
3. **Plain language for busy owners** — No agency jargon; bilingual by default; every section answers “what do I get?”
4. **One clear next step** — Primary CTA leads to contact or a recommended package; secondary paths stay secondary.
5. **Premium without intimidation** — Precise and polished, still approachable for a first-time website buyer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as the bar: sufficient contrast, keyboard-reachable controls, visible focus, labeled form fields, and respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`. Bilingual content must remain equally usable in English and Albanian.
